The Review chain tab names reviewers by vendor and gets its model lists from the providers.

**Update:** the **Update** row in the popover, or
`curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/igor-batrakov/agents-usage/main/install.sh | bash`.
Model lists need a revo with the `revo models` command (`pipx upgrade revolver-review`); with an older revo the
tab works as before, without the update button.

- A reviewer is shown as its vendor: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini. When the chain has two reviewers of one vendor,
  the popover and the Apply confirmation add the model.
- The model menu lists what the providers offer, through `revo models`. Models nobody has reviewed with yet are
  marked. Lists older than a day are fetched when the tab opens; **Update model lists** fetches them all. No
  model is run and nothing is spent.
- The effort list follows what the provider gives the chosen model, and picking a model moves the effort into
  that list.
- The tab shows when the lists were fetched and why a list could not be.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums of both archives.
