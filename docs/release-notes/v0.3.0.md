The Review chain tab for Revolver's flexible chain: required and fallback reviewers.

**Update:** the **Update** row in the popover, or
`curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/igor-batrakov/agents-usage/main/install.sh | bash`.
This version needs revo with JSON schema 2 (`pipx upgrade revolver-review`); with an older revo the tab says so.

- Add, remove and drag reviewers: higher goes first. Each one is **required** (runs on every review, in
  parallel with the other required ones) or a **fallback** (reviews instead of a required one above it, on a
  limit or on any failure). Pick the provider, model and effort for each.
- Fallbacks are shown under the required reviewers and say whom they go for first. Warnings, without blocking:
  no required reviewer is on, a fallback above every required one, a required one with no fallback below.
- Below the list: review rounds per commit with a short explanation, the review language, and what to do when
  a required reviewer gives no review: wait for your decision or commit with a note.
- Apply shows added and removed reviewers along with the other changes before anything is written.
- The popover shows each required reviewer on a limit and who reviews instead.
- If revo is newer than the app, the tab asks you to update the app instead of revo.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums of both archives.
