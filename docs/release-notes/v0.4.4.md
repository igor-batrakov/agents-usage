Fixes from a review of the whole project: safer updates, a cleaner demo, and a handful of small bugs.

**Update:** the **Update** row in the popover, or
`curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/igor-batrakov/agents-usage/main/install.sh | bash`.

- `install.sh` checks the downloaded archive against the release's `SHA256SUMS` and copies the new app next to
  the old one before replacing it: a failed download or copy leaves the installed app in place. Two installs into
  one folder take turns. The in-app updater uses this script from the next update on.
- ↻ and **Check** work right away after you turn off an account or change its folder, key or billing settings
  while it is being polled.
- Google Cloud without a limit gets no empty bar in the menu bar before its first answer. **Check** on a service
  that has only a balance shows the amount instead of "0 windows".
- Demo mode no longer touches your real panel position or the Keychain.
- The review chain tab: the revo path cannot change while a check or a write runs, and the popover's review
  limits line disappears together with its divider when the last limit ends.
- Smaller fixes: an amount the services send as `NaN` or `Infinity` is treated as an error instead of being shown,
  agy window ids no longer depend on the interface language, the release page opens only over https, and the
  billing month follows the Gregorian calendar whatever calendar macOS is set to.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums of both archives.
