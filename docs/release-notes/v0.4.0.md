Google Cloud spending against your own limit, for example only Vertex AI in one project.

**Update:** the **Update** row in the popover, or
`curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/igor-batrakov/agents-usage/main/install.sh | bash`.

- A new service, **Google Cloud**: Settings → Services → **+**. It reads the Standard usage cost export to
  BigQuery (Billing → Billing export) through your `gcloud` login and shows what was spent this month or week.
- Give it the export's `project.dataset`, and optionally one project, one service (Vertex AI) and a limit in the
  billing currency. With a limit it gets a bar that resets with the period, with the same threshold notification
  as the other limits.
- By default it counts the full cost and shows what credits covered separately; **Subtract credits** counts only
  what the card is charged.
- The export lags by a few hours and fills from the day it is turned on; the popover says how fresh the data is.
- The app keeps its one-row query result in a small table in that dataset, so your gcloud account needs write
  access to it. It queries at most every 15 minutes, each query capped at 100 MB: inside BigQuery's free tier.
- Euro amounts show as €.
